My Dream car is BMW

BMW, or Bayerische Motoren Werke AG, is a German multinational company that produces luxury vehicles and motorcycles. Founded in 1916, BMW is known for its high-performance cars, innovative technology, and sleek designs. Some of the popular models produced by BMW include the 3 Series, 5 Series, 7 Series, X5, and X7. The company has a strong presence in motorsports and is considered one of the leading luxury car manufacturers in the world.
